A small toolbox, built to stay out of your way

QuickTools started from a simple annoyance: needing to compress one photo or merge two PDFs, and ending up on a website full of ads, sign-up walls, and confusing settings. This is the opposite of that.

Open, do the thing, done

Every tool follows one flow: open it, upload or type something, process it, download it. No accounts, no wizards.

Your files stay on your device

Tools that can run locally do - using your browser's own file and image APIs. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

Free, genuinely

Nothing here is locked behind a paywall or a "pro" tier. Support is optional and never required to use a tool.

No noise

No ads, no fake reviews, no invented user counts. Just tools that do what they say.

Have a tool you wish existed?

QuickTools grows slowly and deliberately - one well-made tool at a time.
